# Bank transfer APIs

AxiaPay owns the bank directory, verified-beneficiary, quote, consent, transfer identity, state and recovery contracts. This increment supports individual wallet-funded domestic transfers to operator-published **numeric bank account profiles**, with a variable recipient amount and fixed server-owned fee. Account formats, country/currency coverage, per-transfer amount bounds, fees and evidence lifetimes are selected by published immutable profiles/products; no real banks, prices or adapters are seeded. Product maxima enforce configured per-transfer limits. Customer verification tiers, cumulative account limits and launch authorization policy remain release decisions, as in the individual MVP draft.

The feature shares the existing payment instruction and wallet reserve/capture/release journals. It introduces no second spending implementation or duplicated financial state. Bank-specific endpoints check category and ownership within application operations, while the shared payment APIs also support `bank_transfer`. Both surfaces use the same owner/request-key namespace and first-party payment ID. Existing Airtime/Data, TV, electricity, education and insurance fingerprints and history remain unchanged.

## Customer endpoints

All routes require a current bearer session and return `Cache-Control: no-store`. Unknown fields, implicit primitive conversion and numeric account-number conversion are rejected. Exact monetary values use decimal integer strings in currency minor units. Account numbers preserve leading zeros.

| Method | Route                                                      | Responsibility                                                                                                                      |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| GET    | `/v1/bank-transfers/banks`                                 | List enabled immutable bank profiles, filtered by `currency` and `countryCode`, with `limit` 1–100 and `afterId` keyset pagination. |
| GET    | `/v1/bank-transfers/banks/:bankId`                         | Read first-party bank code, display name, currency/country and numeric account length rules.                                        |
| GET    | `/v1/bank-transfers/products`                              | List enabled transfer pricing/routing revisions, filtered by `bankId` and `currency`; pagination is bounded.                        |
| POST   | `/v1/bank-transfers/beneficiary-validations`               | Verify `{ productId, accountNumber }` through a non-spending name enquiry.                                                          |
| GET    | `/v1/bank-transfers/beneficiary-validations/:validationId` | Recover the owner's immutable receiving-account snapshot and expiry.                                                                |
| POST   | `/v1/bank-transfers/quotes`                                | Review `{ walletId, productId, bankBeneficiaryValidationId, amountMinorUnits, narration? }`.                                        |
| GET    | `/v1/bank-transfers/quotes/:quoteId`                       | Read existing owner-scoped terms, with visible expiry and current submission availability.                                          |
| POST   | `/v1/bank-transfers`                                       | Submit or resume `{ quoteId, requestKey }` as explicit consent to the reviewed transfer.                                            |
| GET    | `/v1/bank-transfers/by-request-key?requestKey=...`         | Recover a lost response using the original logical identity without provider I/O.                                                   |
| GET    | `/v1/bank-transfers/:transferId`                           | Read owned transfer, bank-credit confirmation and actual wallet funds progress.                                                     |
| POST   | `/v1/bank-transfers/:transferId/reconcile`                 | Recover the original held transfer through lookup and/or stored-evidence wallet settlement.                                         |

First browse banks and available transfer products, then verify the selected account. An enabled bank profile does not establish provider connectivity; product responses expose `isSubmissionAvailable`. No adapter means no verified beneficiary, transfer acceptance or fresh funds hold. This first version requires authenticated beneficiary verification rather than an optional unverified-recipient path. The adapter verifies the exact product, bank revision, account number and currency before its resolved account name is stored. Invalid, pending, mismatched and timed-out name enquiries cannot be presented as verified. Historical verification remains readable after expiry or bank withdrawal, with no claim of fresh authorization.

The quote states what the recipient receives (`amountMinorUnits`), the server fee (`feeMinorUnits`) and what the wallet pays (`totalDebitMinorUnits`). Beneficiary evidence is bound to owner and selected product/bank; it cannot be reused for another revision or changed account. Quote expiry is the earlier of pricing and name-enquiry lifetimes and is checked again synchronously under wallet locks before a new hold. Narration is optional, at most 140 readable characters, and is preserved in the immutable terms/fingerprint. Direct account/name, price/fee/total, owner, provider and outcome overrides are rejected. Currency and wallet eligibility remain authoritative.

Persist the logical request key before submitting. Reuse the original quote/key after response loss or uncertainty; an unresolved transfer must never be retried as a new instruction automatically. One quote binds to at most one payment, and a key cannot bind to another quote. A deliberate new transfer requires a fresh quote and key. Accepted unfunded instructions recheck current terms/capabilities before a new reservation; expired unreserved quotes need renewed review. Held instructions retain their original recovery obligations after evidence expiry, bank withdrawal or wallet restriction.

## Bank outcome and wallet funds

`transferStatus` reports the shared workflow (`awaiting_funds`, `reserved`, `pending`, `succeeded`, `failed`). Final success/failure requires the corresponding wallet capture/release to commit. `bankOutcome` separately reports authenticated receiving-bank evidence: `unconfirmed`, `credited`, or `failed`. `fundsStatus` derives from the actual matching reservation: `not_reserved`, `reserved`, `debited`, or `released`.

A transfer acknowledgement, provider debit, transport delivery or elapsed timeout cannot prove receiving-account credit. Delayed bank results stay `bankOutcome: unconfirmed` with funds reserved. An independently confirmed failure has a safe `failureReason` (`bank_rejected`, `recipient_unavailable`, `account_restricted`, or the honest generic `transfer_not_completed`), without exposing raw provider messages. An unsafe or unresolved rejection cannot release funds. A bank failure with a wallet-release outage can show `bankOutcome: failed`, `transferStatus: pending` and `fundsStatus: reserved` until the release journal commits; its reason remains observable throughout.

`confirmation` is null until independently authenticated completed recipient credit exists. Its typed fields are `fulfilmentType: bank_transfer`, bank reference `transferReference`, actual UTC `creditedAt`, first-party `bankId`, exact `accountNumber`/`accountName`, recipient `amountMinorUnits` and `currency`. The credit must match the complete reviewed receiving account and principal. A provider success without matching mandatory credit proof is rejected before financial evidence can be recorded or funds captured. Adapter confirmation must represent actual completed receiving-bank credit, not an estimate or a submission acknowledgement.

Bank success evidence and mandatory credit proof are committed in one transaction, with a deferred database constraint preventing partial/incomplete success. A proof-persistence outage rolls the observation back and preserves held funds for reconciliation. Once valid proof exists, wallet settlement can finish after an outage without another provider call, including after adapter or bank withdrawal. Provider movement references are unique per immutable provider realm. Proof and final financial evidence are append-only; competing observations cannot overwrite them. Provider references, evidence, credentials and clearing accounts remain private.

## Application and adapter boundaries

`ServiceCatalogService` owns first-party bank/product discovery through its repository. `BankBeneficiaryService` owns non-financial verification and bounded owner/product evidence. `BankTransferService` owns bank-category review/submission/recovery authorization while coordinating the existing `PaymentQuoteService` and `WalletPaymentService`. `PaymentExecutionService` claims submission exactly once before network I/O, validates correlated observations and stores bank success atomically. Only the wallet feature posts financial journals. Storage clients, provider SDKs and HTTP objects do not enter business workflows.

Registrations use `PaymentsModule.register({ imports, fulfilment: { useFactory, inject } })` or `useValue`/`useExisting`. The [bank name-enquiry port](../../../../../packages/payments-runtime/src/modules/payments/ports/bank-beneficiary-verification.ts) is `bankBeneficiaryVerification.verify`. The shared [transfer execution port](../../../../../packages/payments-runtime/src/modules/payments/ports/service-payment-provider.ts) submits the first-party instruction once and looks up the same identity without initiating another transfer. `termsFingerprint` binds bank revision/account/name/currency, verified snapshot, principal, fee and narration, but it is a correlation value rather than a signature or proof of authenticity. Adapters must authenticate upstream evidence, correlate the receiving account and actual credit, enforce bounded timeouts, and redact personal/financial details. Banking rail/SDK identifiers are adapter-owned translations of first-party bank/profile and product routing.

A bank success observation must supply `fulfilment` with the complete typed credit proof. A confirmed failed result may supply `bankTransferFailureReason`; omission records `transfer_not_completed`. Only independently safe final rejection can set `fundsSafeToRelease: true`. A timeout or missing lookup alone is never final failure. Routing keys isolate stable provider/environment/credential realms and persisted clearing accounts; deployment changes must not reinterpret accepted obligations under a reused key.

This increment implements foreground submission and explicit reconciliation. Durable unattended execution/reconciliation, provider-authenticated webhooks, post-credit reversals/returned-funds accounting, fee-refund policy and bank-transfer SMS eligibility need their own defined workflows before live rollout. A reversal must be a traceable later event, never an overwrite of original credit evidence. The current APIs do not claim reversal/cancellation support or guarantee settlement timing.

## Migration and verification

Apply the migration chain through `202610130008-bank-transfers.js`, then rerun `grant_service_payments_runtime_access('your_runtime_role')` as the migration owner alongside existing wallet/auth grants. Runtime bank publication and financial-history mutation are prohibited. The migration adds immutable bank profiles and beneficiary evidence, bank quote/narration bindings, safe failure classification and mandatory atomic credit-proof protection. It creates no products or adapters and does not change wallet balances. No migration runs automatically on application startup.

Catalogue, beneficiary and financial reads deliberately bypass cache for authoritative publication/eligibility and immediate progress. Operators publish a new revision when account rules, routing or commercial terms change and can withdraw an old one without altering historical transfer snapshots.

The integration suite exercises real HTTP authentication/validation and restricted PostgreSQL access using test-only adapters. It covers name enquiry correlation, leading zeros, privacy, money/limits/currency, beneficiary and quote expiry under wallet locks, competing reservations, submission deduplication across APIs, uncertain outcomes, atomic proof rollback, confirmed safe releases and bank/ledger outages. Upgrade tests retain existing mobile, TV and insurance consent/policy/document history and fingerprints. Root composition continues to register no provider adapters.
